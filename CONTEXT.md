# Kya PC port

Glossary for the Kya: Dark Lineage decompilation, PC port and remaster planning.

## Language

**Enhanced port**:
The original game running on the decompilation with modern resolution, widescreen, frame rate, controls, menus and quality-of-life features, using the original assets.
_Avoid_: port (alone), HD version

**Remaster**:
The enhanced port plus reworked assets (upscaled textures, cleaned audio), shipped as optional asset packs, keeping the original art style.
_Avoid_: remake, HD remake

**Remake**:
A rebuild of the game in a modern engine. Comparison only, not a planned route.

**Visual preset**:
A single switch between **Original** (original look and timing) and **Enhanced** (all rendering upgrades), on top of per-feature toggles.

**Quality-of-life feature** (QoL):
A change to controls, camera, saves, menus, cutscenes or accessibility that does not alter game balance.
_Avoid_: difficulty assist (that is rebalancing, out of scope)

**Stand-in**:
An original actor, level or cinematic that plays a sequel role without changing its asset, reframed only through position and text (e.g. Frank-Wolfen standing in for the keepers' chief).
_Avoid_: reskin, replacement

## Relationships

- The **Remaster** is an **Enhanced port** plus asset packs; the enhanced port ships first.
- **QoL features** never change balance; anything that does is rebalancing.
